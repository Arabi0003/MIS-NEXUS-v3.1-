Quick instructions

How to create a boarder QR

  1. Register the boarder in your boarders system.
  2. Copy the student's actual student_code.
  3. Enter that value into Student Code / QR ID.
  4. Enter the student's name and other information.
  5. Upload the school logo.
  6. Upload the student's photograph.
  7. Press Generate QR.
  8. Download or print the card.
  9. The QR should contain only the attendance identifier.
